How much would proven work save you?

A one-minute estimate for your portfolio: the staff time Hey Helpy takes over (sending work orders, collecting photos and acts, reconciling the monthly statement) plus the contractor overbilling you stop paying for, because work without proof is not paid. We use cautious defaults: 3% overbilling, adjustable from 3% to 10%.

How we calculate

  1. Staff time. Hours saved per site per month (by default 15 coordinator hours and 5 facility manager hours) × the cost of an hour. The cost of an hour = annual salary × 1.25 (taxes and social contributions) / 2,080 working hours.
  2. Cut overbilling. Contractor spend per site per month × the overbilling share. By default, contractor spend = site area × repair and maintenance cost per m² per year × 70% (the share that goes to contractors) / 12. You can enter your own figure.
  3. Benefit = staff time + cut overbilling, multiplied by the number of sites.
  4. Hey Helpy cost = subscription for the site’s area tier (−15% for annual payment) + the optional payout fee, multiplied by the number of sites.
  5. Net benefit = benefit − cost; per year = × 12. Return = benefit / cost, shown as “×2.6”. The range is the same calculation at 3% and at 10% overbilling.
  6. Money is rounded to whole units of currency, the return to one decimal place. Sites over 30,000 m² have custom pricing — we do not estimate them.
