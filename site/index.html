Hey Helpy — for business and shopping centres, chains and retail, property management and FM companies, public-sector customers

AI service for property maintenance and contractor control

Pay contractors only for proven work. A work order is created by text, camera or voice — in the app or via Telegram, with nothing to install. The service figures out what broke and where, sends it to the right contractor and collects proof of completion.

How many people does it take to fix one lock?

An employee posts in a group chat. The receptionist calls the dispatcher. The dispatcher looks up which contractor handles doors at this site. The manager sends reminders. The contractor shows up when they can and sends an invoice. Accounting pays — on trust.

Everyone does their part, yet in the end nobody can show that the work was done, where, when and by whom. Requests get lost in chats, overdue work only surfaces through complaints, and whatever is on the invoice gets paid.

How it works

  1. Intake

    Say it: “Hey, Helpy, water is dripping from the air conditioner in the third-floor meeting room.” Or point the camera at the equipment. Or message the Telegram bot — requesters need no app, just the QR code at the site.

  2. AI parses it

    YandexGPT works out what happened, in which room, which building system it is and how urgent. From a photo, the service finds the exact piece of equipment in the asset register. A person just confirms.

  3. Contractor

    The work order goes straight to the contractor assigned to that system at that site. The technician gets a notification and takes it with one tap.

  4. Proof

    The “after” photo can only be taken with the app’s live camera and carries a stamp: date · site · room · inside geofence. The visit is checked against the site’s geofence, the checklist item by item. No “after” photo — no hand-over.

  5. Contractor act

    At month-end the contractor act is built automatically: only accepted work with proof is payable, disputed items go to a manager’s decision, the rest show why they are not payable.

Highlights

Camera assistant

Work orders from Telegram

“Before” and “after” photos

The work order holds all the proof: photos before and after, time, place, documents.
